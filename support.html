Getting started

  1. Open a recipe from the home screen.
  2. Tap Start cooking. The first time, iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition access.
  3. Say "Yes" to begin. YumYum reads the intro, the ingredients and the first step.

Commands

  • Nextthe next step
  • Backthe step before
  • Repeatread this step again
  • Ingredientswhat you need right now
  • Wait / Keep goingpause and pick up
  • Faster / Slowerchange the reading pace
  • Stop cookingend the session after you confirm

Also: "Normal speed" resets the reading pace; "Done" also moves to the next step; "Go back" works better than "Back" in a noisy kitchen.

Troubleshooting

  • Voice control won't start: open iOS Settings > YumYum and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  • YumYum says voice isn't available: your iPhone needs on-device English speech recognition. If it isn't available, YumYum keeps voice off and you can cook with the buttons.
  • It misses a word: speak toward the phone and try the longer form ("Go back", "Say that again").
  • No timer sound: with the app open, the chime plays even in Silent mode. With the screen off, iOS plays the notification sound, and Silent mode or a Focus can mute it. Check Settings > Notifications > YumYum.

About the recipes

Check the allergen line on each recipe before you start. Cooking times are guides; check that meat and seafood are cooked through.
