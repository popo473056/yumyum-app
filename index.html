Say it and the recipe moves

Open the cooking screen and YumYum starts listening. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone, and every command has a button too.

  • Nextthe next step
  • Backthe step before
  • Repeatread this step again
  • Ingredientswhat you need right now
  • Wait / Keep goingpause and pick up
  • Faster / Slowerchange the reading pace
  • Stop cookingend the session after you confirm

Doenjang? Gochugaru? Tap it.

Each ingredient opens to show its Korean name, what it is in plain English, and a swap you can find at a regular grocery store when one exists.

Timers that keep counting when you walk away

A step with a simmer time starts its own timer. Move on to chopping, and the timer keeps going. You hear a chime when it ends, even with the screen off.
