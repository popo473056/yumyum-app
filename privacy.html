Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

YumYum is a cooking app made by an independent developer. This policy covers YumYum for iPhone, version 1.0.

What YumYum collects

Nothing. YumYum has no account and no ads, and this version sends no analytics. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you turn on voice control, YumYum uses the microphone on the cooking screen only. Apple's speech recognition turns your words into text on your iPhone, with on-device recognition required. YumYum matches that text against a fixed list of cooking commands and then discards it. The app does not record, store or send your audio or transcripts. You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in iOS Settings > YumYum; the app keeps working with on-screen buttons.

Notifications

Step timers use local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows. No server is involved.

Information stored on your iPhone

YumYum keeps a few preferences on your device, such as your reading speed and whether you have seen the introduction. Deleting the app deletes them.

Links to other sites

Recipe pages link to the original photo pages on Wikimedia Commons. Those open in your browser, and Wikimedia's privacy policy applies there.

Children

YumYum does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version collects any data, this page will say what and why before that version ships, and the app will ask you first. The effective date above shows the latest change.
